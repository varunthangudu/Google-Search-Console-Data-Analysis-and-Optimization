The First Lakh — money basics for your first job.

The First Lakh is an educational content site built for a Google Search Console capstone project submitted to Unified Mentor. It explains how financial products work; it does not recommend any specific fund, insurer, bank or investment, and nothing here is personalised financial advice. Tax figures are stated for FY 2025-26 and change with each Budget — verify current rules at incometax.gov.in or with a registered adviser before acting.
